About

CCCC (Conservative Classification of Creative Content) lets viewers, reviewers, creators and anyone else rate creative works by what they contain, not by age.

Why we built it

Age ratings tell you which age a work suits, not what's in it. Conservative audiences don't ask "is this fine for 13 and up?" — they ask "is it free of blasphemy, magic and nudity?" Existing rating systems don't answer that, especially for anime, manga and games, so we built a tool that does.

How it works

Each content flag has one of three levels, based on how prominent it is and how easily it can be avoided:

  • Level 1 · Core
  • Level 2 · Recurring
  • Level 3 · Passing

Music, profanity and romantic relationships outside marriage have no levels. Use an exception badge to show that a work is free of one of them.

See the rating guide for details.

What we're not

We are not a censorship body and do not issue binding rulings. We provide the system and the tool. Users rate works they have watched, read or played, publish ratings in their own name, and take responsibility for them.

Open to everyone

The project is open source, and anyone can use it and share their ratings.

Data sources

Titles, details and images come from TMDB (movies and TV), RAWG, SteamGridDB and HowLongToBeat (games), AniList and Kitsu (anime and manga), and Open Library (books). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Privacy

We count visits anonymously with GoatCounter: no cookies, no personal data, just how many people visit.

    • LevelsRenamed to describe how present the content is, not how strong: Core, Recurring and Passing (formerly Severe, Moderate and Mild). Level 1 now also covers content that is present throughout a work.
    • BlasphemyNow includes mocking the prophets, advocating beliefs that conflict with Islam, and (in games) the player worshipping anything other than God.
