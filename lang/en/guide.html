Levels

LEVEL SCALE · 03 LEVELS
Presence scale Passing Core
1
Level 1
Core
In story-based media

The content is central and appears frequently, so skipping it makes the story hard to follow; or it is present throughout, such as a character shown in revealing clothes in most scenes.

In games

You cannot finish the game without encountering this content.

2
Level 2
Recurring
In story-based media

You can skip the content and still follow the story, but frequent skipping disrupts the experience.

In games

You can finish the game while avoiding this content, but doing so makes it harder and changes the experience.

3
Level 3
Passing
In story-based media

The content is rare and easy to skip without losing track of the story.

In games

You can ignore the content or take another route to finish the game.

Content flags

10 CONTENT FLAGS · 3 GROUPS
02Belief and ideology 05Moral and disturbing content 03Spending and engagement
[01]Belief Blasphemy

Content that mocks God, the prophets or religion; advocates disbelief, atheism or beliefs that conflict with Islam; or (in games) has the player worship anything other than God, such as praying, bowing or making offerings.

[02]Belief Magic

Powers, symbols and spells tied to sorcery, astrology and fortune-telling.

[03]Moral Sexual content

Sexual acts, shown or clearly implied, such as a scene that cuts away just before or after. Kissing is not included (it counts under Vices).

[04]Moral Nudity

Showing parts of the body that are normally covered: from revealing clothes such as swimwear, underwear or visible cleavage, to partial or full nudity. For men: anything between the navel and the knee.

[05]Moral LGBTQ+ themes

Depictions or positive portrayals of LGBTQ+ themes, including same-sex relationships and transgender identities.

[06]Moral Disturbing

Graphic violence, gore, torture, suicide and rape, and cruelty the player carries out themselves.

[07]Moral Vices

Drinking, drug use or gambling on screen, or promoting them, plus seduction: kissing, seductive talk or gestures, and shots that dwell on the body. Smoking is not included, as it appears in most works.

[08]Games only Addictive design

(Games only) Mechanics that require regular daily returns to keep progressing, such as daily logins and streaks.

[09]Games only Pay-to-win

(Games only) Progress is tied to money spent on in-game items.

[10]Games only Gambling

(Games only) Mechanics that encourage spending on loot boxes or gacha pulls with random rewards.

Exceptions

EXCEPTION BADGES · +03

For rating purposes, CCCC assumes a work contains music or songs, profanity, and romantic relationships outside marriage unless marked otherwise. Use each badge below only when the work is free of that content.
